Activities

Every run, ride and swim, in detail.

All your activities in one fast table, back to the very first. Open one for its route, heart rate, pace and elevation, splits that match your watch's laps, your fastest stretches, heart rate zones and a coach's take that knows a tempo session from an easy jog.

Sleep

Last night, and the nights before it.

Your stages against the optimal ranges, heart rate through the night, sleep debt against the sleep your watch says you need, how regular your bedtimes are, and your body clock: how much later you sleep at weekends, and whether you're an early bird or a night owl.

HRV

Recovery, read like a sports scientist would.

Your 7-day HRV against a normal range from the 60 days before, how much it swings from day to day, how it moves with your resting heart rate, and what tends to come before a low or a high morning: hard days, late nights, short sleep.

Records

Your fastest 5K is hiding in a long run.

Splits finds your best 1 km, 5 km, 10 km, half and marathon inside every outdoor run, shows how each record came down over the years, and predicts what you'd run if you raced today.

Explorer

Everywhere you've run.

Every country and city you've been active in, joined up in the order you went, year by year. Places are found on your Mac from bundled maps, so where you run never leaves it.

  • Trends A year of days at a glance, streaks, this year against last, weekly load and aerobic form.
  • Morning briefing How recovered you are and what today calls for, written once last night's sleep syncs.
  • Menu bar and widgets Energy, stress and heart rate at a glance, and your week on the desktop.
  • Share cards A picture of a route with no map underneath, so your street stays yours.

Private by design.

Your key, your data

Splits uses a Personal API key you create in your own Garmin Connect account. It lives in your Mac's keychain and is only ever sent to that API, to fetch your own data.

No server, no tracking

There's no Splits account and no Splits server. Everything it downloads stays on your Mac. No analytics, no ads, and this site has no cookies either.

AI that stays home

The coach's notes are written by Apple's on-device model, on your Mac. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI. The verdicts come from the numbers; the model only puts them into words.

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download Splits

    Unzip it and move Splits to your Applications folder. It's signed and notarized by Apple, and updates itself.

  2. 2

    Create a key

    In Garmin Connect on the web, open Settings › Personal API Key, create one and copy it.

  3. 3

    Paste it in

    Splits fetches your history, all of it, and keeps up from then on.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases. Splits is open source under the MIT License.

Which watches work?

Any watch or device whose data reaches your Garmin Connect account. Splits shows what your device records; not every device records everything.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later; it's tested on macOS 26 and later. The coach's notes need macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else works without it.

Why a Personal API key?

It's how you give your own app access to your own data, without a password and without a server in between. You can delete the key in Garmin Connect at any time, and Splits stops.

Is it medical advice?

No. Splits is not a medical device. Its numbers and notes are general fitness information and can be wrong. Talk to a doctor about anything health related.
